## How to Play

In **Planet Clicker**, each click on your planet produces valuable resources, enabling you to invest in upgrades that enhance your civilization. Construct buildings, improve infrastructure, and advance technology—all while strategically allocating resources to maximize growth and efficiency.

## Key Features

- **Resource Management**: Accumulate coins, energy, and population through clicking and smart upgrades.
- **Automation**: Unlock features for passive resource generation, reducing the need for constant clicking.
- **Exploration**: Discover new planets, moons, and celestial bodies to expand your empire and gather diverse resources.
- **Customization**: Personalize your planet’s appearance with continuous upgrades and unique options.
- **Challenges & Events**: Tackle obstacles and participate in events that enrich your gameplay experience.
- **Competitive Elements**: Climb the leaderboards and compete with other players to showcase your achievements.

## Progression and Community

As you evolve from a small colony to a thriving interstellar civilization, engage with a vibrant community, share strategies, and celebrate your successes. Regular updates keep the content fresh and the gameplay exciting.
